Visit India is an all in one travel guide for travellers and people searching for information about India.

Destinations

India is well known for its beautiful scenery and rich culture.

  • Delhi – The capital city of India. It has a lot of history, busy streets, markets, and different types of food.
  • Agra – Famous for the Taj Mahal. Many tourists visit Agra as part of a short trip.
  • Jaipur – Known for its forts and palaces. It is sometimes called the Pink City.
  • Goa – A popular place for beaches and a more relaxed type of holiday.

Culture

  • People are expected to dress modestly in religious places
  • Shoes are often removed before entering temples
  • It is polite to ask before taking photos of people
  • Customs can be different depending on the area

Travel Tips

  • visa requirements are checked before travelling
  • Train tickets should be booked in advance where possible
  • It is useful to carry some cash as well as a card
  • Important documents should be backed up
