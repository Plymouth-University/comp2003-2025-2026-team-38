Welcome

Visit India is an all-in-one travel guide for travellers and people searching for information about India. Explore the rich history, vibrant culture, and breathtaking landscapes. India has 29 states, a population of 1.4 billion people, and over 3000 years of history.

From the snowy peaks of the Himalayas to the tropical beaches of Goa, India offers something for every type of traveller. Whether you are drawn by the ancient temples, the bustling cities, or the incredible food, no two trips to India are ever the same.

Popular Destinations

India is well known for its beautiful scenery and rich culture.

  • Delhi

    The capital city of India. It has a lot of history, busy streets, markets, and different types of food.

  • Agra

    Famous for the Taj Mahal. Many tourists visit Agra as part of a short trip.

  • Jaipur

    Known for its forts and palaces. It is sometimes called the Pink City.

  • Goa

    A popular place for beaches and a more relaxed type of holiday.

Culture & Etiquette

India has a rich and varied culture. Customs can differ between regions, but these general tips will help you show respect during your visit.

Religion & Dress

  • Dress modestly when visiting temples, mosques, or other religious sites.
  • Remove your shoes before entering temples and many private homes.
  • Some sites require your head to be covered — carry a scarf just in case.

Social Customs

  • Always ask before taking photos of people.
  • The head is considered sacred — avoid touching someone's head.
  • Use your right hand for eating, giving, and receiving items.

Food & Drink

  • Many Indians are vegetarian — vegetarian options are widely available.
  • Avoid drinking tap water. Stick to bottled or filtered water.
  • Street food is popular but choose busy stalls with high turnover for safety.

Travel Tips

India is a vast country, some general travel tipss .

Before You Go

  • Check visa requirements well in advance — most visitors need an e-Visa.
  • Speak to your doctor about recommended vaccinations before travelling.
  • Back up important documents like your passport and travel insurance.

Getting Around

  • Book train tickets early — routes between major cities fill up fast.
  • Use reputable apps like Ola or Uber in cities rather than unmarked taxis.
  • Domestic flights are affordable and save time on longer journeys.

Money & Staying Connected

  • Carry some cash — many smaller shops and markets do not accept cards.
  • Pick up a local SIM card at the airport for cheap data and calls.
  • Bargaining is normal in markets but always stay polite and friendly.
